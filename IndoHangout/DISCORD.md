## 🎣 IndoHangout — Automation Suite `v1.0.0`

Auto fishing, auto mining, movement tools and saved profiles for **IndoHangout** — all in one clean, lightweight UI.

### 📥 Load

```lua
loadstring(game:HttpGet("https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LyraTheGuy/XRoblox/staging/IndoHangout/bootstrap.lua"))()
```

### 🐟 Fishing
**Auto Fish** — casts, reels and catches for you.
**Streak Protection** — saves your streak the moment progress dips below your threshold.
**Auto Sell** — **per count** or **per minute**, your choice, with a **Sell Category** picker (Sell All / under 50 / 400 / 600 Kg). **Protected Fish** are never sold.
**Locked Spot** — every cast starts from the position you saved.
Live stats: catches / in bag / streak saves.

### ⛏️ Mining
**Auto Mine** — equips your **best pickaxe** (or the one you choose), glides to a random crystal in range and **locks onto it**, swinging every 1.5s until it drops into your bag (up to 6 swings, retrying any missed hit).
**Auto Sell** — every X crystals, with its own cooldown and the same Sell Category picker.

### 🕹️ Misc
**Fly** (camera-relative, WASD + Space/Ctrl, adjustable speed), **WalkSpeed**, **Noclip**. Everything restores on unload and re-applies after respawn.

### 💾 Settings
Save / load / delete JSON profiles plus autoload on execute — fishing settings, protected fish and locked spot all carry over.

### 🎛️ UI
Sidebar tabs (About · Fishing · Mining · Misc · Settings), draggable window, minimize chip and a live status strip. Re-executing replaces the old copy instead of stacking duplicates.
